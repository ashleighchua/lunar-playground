What is Astrocartography?

Your birth chart captures the positions of the planets at the exact moment you were born. But if you had been born at that same moment in a different location, the planets would have appeared in different positions relative to you.

Astrocartography maps these differences across the globe. It shows where each planet was rising, setting, or at its peak at your birth moment. When you visit or live near one of these lines, that planet's themes become more active in your life.

Quick Reference: The Four Angles

AC (Ascendant)
— The eastern horizon. Planets here affect how you present yourself and how others first perceive you.
DC (Descendant)
— The western horizon. Planets here influence partnerships, relationships, and one-on-one connections.
MC (Midheaven)
— The highest point in the sky. Planets here shape your public image, career, and visibility.
IC (Imum Coeli)
— The lowest point. Planets here affect your inner world, home life, and private self.

The Planetary Lines in This Reading

Neptune MC (Midheaven)

Neptune IC (Imum Coeli)

Sun MC (Midheaven)

The Sun on the Midheaven can bring themes of visibility and recognition. Your authentic self may become more publicly known. Creative work tends to get noticed here.
