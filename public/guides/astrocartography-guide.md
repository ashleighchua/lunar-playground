# Part I: Foundations

---

## 1. What Is Astrocartography

Astrocartography (also called relocational astrology or astro-mapping) is a branch of astrology that maps your birth chart onto the globe. Rather than examining planets in houses and signs as in a standard natal chart, astrocartography shows where specific planetary energies are strongest *geographically*.

### Origin

The technique was developed by astrologer **Jim Lewis** in the 1970s. Lewis took centuries of locational astrology theory and turned it into a visual, practical tool: a world map overlaid with personalised planetary lines. He coined the term Astro\*Carto\*Graphy, built the first software with programmer Michael Erlewine, and received a patent for his overlay method in 1981. His book *The Psychology of Astro\*Carto\*Graphy* (co-written with Kenneth Irving) remains a foundational text. He received the Regulus Award for Discovery, Innovation, and Research in 1992. This technique has been studied and practiced for decades.

### How It Works

The planets were in specific positions when you were born. Depending on where you are on Earth, those planets align with different angular points in your chart. Some cities activate your Venus line, which governs love and connection. Some activate your Saturn line, which brings structure and harder lessons. The map shows you all of it at once.

Your birth chart is calculated for your birth *location*. When you move, the planets shift position relative to your new location. Astrocartography visualises that shift.

### Applications

| Use case | What astrocartography reveals |
|---|---|
| Relocation planning | Which cities align with your career, relationship, or personal growth priorities |
| Travel | Why certain destinations resonate more strongly than others |
| Retrospective analysis | Why you thrived in one city and struggled in another |
| Decision support | An additional data point when weighing relocation options |

This is not about finding one "perfect" city. Every location has trade-offs. The goal is to understand what each place activates so you can choose consciously.

## 3. Anatomy of the Map

When you open your astrocartography map, you'll see a world map with coloured lines running across it. Some are straight (vertical), some are curved. Each line represents a planet, and each line type represents an angle.

### What You're Seeing

**Lines are colour-coded by planet.** Each planet gets its own colour (varies by tool, always labelled). You'll see lines for all major celestial bodies: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto.

**Each planet has four lines**, one per angle:

| Line type | Angle | Appearance |
|---|---|---|
| MC | Midheaven | Vertical (straight) |
| IC | Imum Coeli / Nadir | Vertical (straight) |
| AC | Ascendant | Curved |
| DC | Descendant | Curved |

10 planets x 4 lines = 40 lines on your map. You don't need to read all 40 at once.

### Where to Focus First

Start with the planet that matches your current priority:

| Priority | Focus on |
|---|---|
| Love and relationships | ♀ Venus and ☽ Moon lines |
| Career and public life | ☉ Sun and ♃ Jupiter lines |
| Growth and opportunity | ♃ Jupiter lines |
| Communication and learning | ☿ Mercury lines |
| Drive and ambition | ♂ Mars lines |
| Home and emotional wellbeing | ☽ Moon lines |

Find those lines on your map. Note which cities and countries they pass through.

## 4. The Four Angles

The angles are the backbone of astrocartography. They determine *how* a planet's energy shows up in a location.

The planet is the *what*. The angle is the *where in your life*.

---

### MC: Midheaven

**Domain:** Career, public life, reputation
**Line type:** Vertical
**Visibility:** Very public

The Midheaven is the highest point in your chart. It represents your public face, career, reputation, and how the world sees you.

MC lines run straight up and down, marking the longitude where a planet was at its highest point in the sky at the moment of your birth. When a planet is on your MC in a location, that planet's energy is front and centre in your professional and public life there.

**MC energy manifests as:** being seen, being known for something, career activation, public recognition, stepping into a visible role.

---

### IC: Imum Coeli (Nadir)

**Domain:** Home, roots, inner life
**Line type:** Vertical
**Visibility:** Very private

The IC is the deepest, most private point in your chart, opposite the MC. It represents your inner world, your sense of home, your roots, and your emotional foundation.

IC lines run vertically, exactly 180 degrees from the MC line. When a planet is on your IC, that energy operates in your private life. It shapes how you feel at home, your relationship with family, and your inner emotional landscape.

**IC energy manifests as:** coming home, emotional depth, private transformation, inner knowing, ancestral connection.

> The IC is frequently overlooked. It shouldn't be. Where you feel genuinely at home affects everything else. A strong IC placement can be the foundation that makes career, relationships, and personal growth possible.

---

### AC: Ascendant

**Domain:** Self-image, identity, first impressions
**Line type:** Curved
**Visibility:** Personal, visible to others

The Ascendant is your rising sign energy: how you come across to others, your outward personality, and the lens through which you experience the world.

AC lines are curved because they trace where a planet was rising on the eastern horizon. The curve occurs because the horizon angle changes with latitude. (Technically: AC lines are computed from the hour angle and declination of each planet at each latitude. MC/IC lines are simpler, based purely on longitude.)

When a planet is on your AC, it colours your entire self-expression in that location.

**AC energy manifests as:** shifts in self-perception, changed personal magnetism, feeling like a different version of yourself.

---

### DC: Descendant

**Domain:** Relationships, partnerships, one-on-one connections
**Line type:** Curved
**Visibility:** Experienced through others

The Descendant is opposite the Ascendant. It represents other people, partnerships, and what you attract. This includes romantic partners, business partners, close friends, and anyone you form a significant one-on-one bond with.

DC lines are curved, opposite the AC curves. When a planet is on your DC, it shapes the kind of people you attract and the quality of your relationships in that location.

**DC energy manifests as:** attracting a particular type of person, relationship shifts, significant encounters, changed partnership dynamics.

---

### Angle Hierarchy

Among practitioners, the angles are generally ranked by intensity:

| Rank | Angle | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **MC** | Governs your most visible, public-facing life. Strongest angular point in traditional astrology. |
| 2 | **AC** | Fundamentally shifts how you present yourself and how others perceive you. |
| 3 | **DC** | Directly shapes relationship quality and partner attraction. |
| 4 | **IC** | Quietest in terms of external visibility, but arguably the most foundational. |

Intensity and importance are not the same thing. Your IC line might not be the loudest, but it could be the one that matters most.

### Planetary Weight

Not all lines carry equal weight. In practice, most relocational astrologers rank planetary impact roughly as follows:

| Tier | Planets | Relative Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Highest** | ☉ Sun, ☽ Moon | Most personally impactful |
| **High** | ♀ Venus, ♃ Jupiter | Traditional "benefics" |
| **Moderate** | ♂ Mars, ♄ Saturn | Significant but more challenging |
| **Lower** | ☿ Mercury | Important for communication/intellect specifically |
| **Contextual** | ♅ ♆ ♇ | Slower-moving, generational, subtler effects |

Context matters. A writer evaluating cities for a writing career should weight ☿ Mercury MC more heavily than this general ranking suggests.

> This guide focuses primarily on ☉ ☽ ☿ ♀ ♂ ♃ ♄. These are the most personally relevant planets. Your map includes all of them, and you now know what each one brings.

## 7. Orbs and Distance

You don't have to be *exactly* on a line for it to affect you. The influence fades gradually with distance. This zone of influence is called the "orb."

### What the Experts Say

There is active debate among practitioners about effective orb distance:

| Source | Recommended Orb | Equivalent Distance |
|---|---|---|
| **Jim Lewis** (original technique) | 8-12 degrees | ~550-830 miles |
| **Helena Woods** (modern practitioner) | 4-8 degrees | ~350-600 miles |
| **Contemporary consensus** | 4-6 degrees for strong effects | ~200-350 miles |
| **Client/community reports** (Astrocartology forums) | Tightest ranges | Intense within 25 mi, strong within 50, noticeable within 100 |

### Working Framework

| Distance from line | Strength | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **0-50 miles** (0-80 km) | Very strong | Unmistakable influence |
| **50-150 miles** (80-240 km) | Strong | Clearly noticeable, significant |
| **150-350 miles** (240-560 km) | Moderate | Present but softer, background influence |
| **350-600 miles** (560-960 km) | Mild | Lewis would say active; many modern astrologers say fading |
| **600+ miles** (960+ km) | Negligible | Most practitioners would not factor this in |

### Practical Implications

- A city 50 miles from your ♃ Jupiter MC line is a very strong ♃ Jupiter MC location
- You don't need to be exactly on the line. Within 150 miles, you're solidly in the zone
- Benefics (♃ Jupiter, ♀ Venus) may have wider effective ranges than malefics (♂ Mars, ♄ Saturn), though this is observational, not proven

### Distance as Intensity

Being right on a line means the energy is at full strength. For ♃ Jupiter and ♀ Venus, that's generally welcome. For ♂ Mars or ♄ Saturn, full strength can be overwhelming. Some practitioners recommend living 150-300 miles from a challenging line to benefit from the energy without being consumed by it.

Think of distance as a volume dial. Full volume isn't always what serves you best.

## 8. Line Crossings and Power Zones

A **line crossing** occurs when two planetary lines intersect at the same location. Both planetary energies activate simultaneously, combining and amplifying each other.

### Technical Background: Parans

The technical term for these crossings is **parans** (short for paranatellontae). When two lines cross, they create a horizontal latitude line extending east-west from the crossing point. The combined energy runs along that entire latitude, though it is strongest at and near the actual intersection, with an orb of approximately 1 degree of latitude (~70 miles north or south).

Parans are considered very powerful in locational astrology. Even without major planetary lines in your area, you almost certainly have paran lines nearby.

### How to Identify Crossings

On Astro.com's Astro Click Travel, click directly on crossing points to see which planets are involved. Astro-Seek also displays paran lines clearly.

### Favourable Crossings

| Crossing | Combined Energy |
|---|---|
| ♀ + ♃ | Love + luck + beauty + abundance. One of the most favourable crossings possible. |
| ☉ + ♃ | Career success + expansion. Excellent for professional growth. |
| ☽ + ♀ | Emotional comfort + beauty + love. Strong for home and relationships. |
| ☿ + ♃ | Ideas + opportunity. Strong for entrepreneurs, writers, communicators. |
| ♀ + ☽ | Love + emotional depth. Relationships feel both beautiful and nourishing. |

### Challenging Crossings

| Crossing | Combined Energy |
|---|---|
| ♂ + ♄ | Drive + restriction. Hard work, sometimes frustrating. What you build is solid. |
| ♂ + ♇ | Intense power dynamics. Transformative but potentially volatile. |
| ♄ + ♇ | Deep transformation through difficulty. Life-changing but heavy. |
| ♆ + any | Inspiring and disorienting. Dreamlike quality but reduced clarity. |

### The Power Zone

The area within ~100-150 miles of a crossing is the "power zone." Both energies are active here. The city closest to the intersection feels both influences most strongly.

> **From client work:** A client had ♀ Venus DC and ♃ Jupiter AC lines crossing through Eastern China. Romantic connection (♀ Venus DC) was supported by warmth and openness (♃ Jupiter AC). They also had ♄ Saturn AC running through the same region, adding seriousness. The read: love here wouldn't be effortless, but what developed would be meaningful. ♃ Jupiter made them approachable, ♀ Venus made connection likely, ♄ Saturn ensured it wasn't superficial.

## 9. The Relocated Chart

This is the difference between a surface-level reading and understanding what a city will actually feel like day to day.

### What Is a Relocated Chart?

Your natal chart is calculated for your birth location. A relocated chart recalculates the same chart as if you were born at the exact same moment, but in a different city. The planets stay in the same signs (your cosmic blueprint doesn't change), but the **houses and angles shift**.

As astrologer Alice Bell explains: while the astrocartography map only shows when planets meet up with the four angles, the relocation chart shows what houses all your planets shift into, even if they aren't touching angles. This is a crucial distinction.

Houses determine *where in your life* planets express themselves. A planet in your 12th house (hidden, private) behaves very differently from the same planet in your 1st house (visible, front and centre) or your 7th house (through partnerships).

### Astrocartography vs. Relocated Chart

| | Astrocartography | Relocated Chart |
|---|---|---|
| **Shows** | Where planets meet the 4 angles | Where all planets land in houses |
| **Scope** | Global overview | City-specific detail |
| **Best for** | Identifying promising regions | Evaluating a specific city deeply |
| **Analogy** | Satellite view | Street-level view |

### How to Generate

**Astro.com:** Go to your natal chart and change the birth location to your target city. Keep date and time the same.

**Astro-Seek.com:** Use the dedicated "Relocation Chart" tool.

### What to Examine

| Element | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **New Ascendant** | How you come across in this city. Your outward energy shifts. |
| **Moon's house** | Where you find emotional comfort. 4th = home/family. 7th = partnership. 11th = community. |
| **Sun's house** | Where your identity expresses most naturally. 10th = career-central. 12th = introspective. |
| **New MC sign** | What kind of career energy the city supports. |
| **Planets in 7th house** | Directly shapes relationship experience. ☽ Moon = emotional partnerships. ♄ Saturn = committed but slow. ♆ Neptune = fated but clarity needed. |

### When to Use It

For a quick check, the astrocartography map is sufficient. For a serious relocation decision, pull up the relocated chart as well. It reveals things the map alone cannot.

## 11. The Relocation Decision Framework

### Step 1: Define Your Priority

What is your primary goal for your next chapter? You can want more than one thing, but knowing which one leads helps you weight the lines.

| Priority | Best lines |
|---|---|
| Career advancement | ☉ MC · ♃ MC · ♂ MC |
| Finding love | ♀ DC · ♀ AC · ☽ DC |
| Feeling at home | ☽ IC · ♀ IC · ♃ IC |
| Personal growth | ♃ AC · ☉ AC |
| Creative expression | ♀ MC · ♆ AC · ☉ AC |
| Building wealth | ♃ MC · ☉ MC · ♄ MC (long game) |
| Intellectual stimulation | ☿ MC · ☿ AC · ♃ AC |
| Fresh start | ♅ AC · ♇ AC · ☉ AC |

### Step 2: Three-City Method

Identify your top three cities based on priority lines. For each:

**Map out:**
- Active lines (planet + angle)
- Distance from each line
- Any crossings
- Overall "signature" from interpretations

**Score:**
- Alignment with your #1 priority (strong / moderate / weak)
- Challenging lines present (noted honestly)
- Practical feasibility (visa, cost, language, climate)

### Step 3: Practical Overlay

Astrocartography is one input, not the only one. A city with ideal planetary alignment but no job market in your field, an impossible visa situation, or a climate that genuinely doesn't suit you is still not the right city.

Use astrocartography as a **filter and a tiebreaker**:

1. **Start with your practical shortlist** — cities you could actually move to
2. **Overlay your astrocartography** — which of these have the best activations?
3. **Visit first if possible** — spend a week or two there
4. **Trust your body** — if a city with great lines doesn't feel right, that's data too

### A Note on Timing

Planetary transits interact with your astrocartography lines. A city might feel average most years but activate powerfully during a specific transit period. If you work with transit astrology, layer it on top of your astrocartography reading for additional nuance.

## 14. Common Mistakes

These come up often in client work. Worth knowing before you start making decisions.

**1. Ignoring birth time accuracy**
A 30-minute error can displace your AC and DC lines significantly. MC and IC lines are affected too, to a lesser degree. Get the most accurate birth time available before making decisions.

**2. Only looking at favourable lines**
Everyone gravitates toward their ♃ Jupiter MC. That's natural. But ignoring ♄ Saturn or ♂ Mars lines in a city means you're only seeing half the picture. Check all lines near any city you're seriously considering.

**3. Treating one line as deterministic**
A city with your ♀ Venus DC doesn't guarantee love. It means the *conditions* are more favourable. Astrocartography shows potential, not promises.

**4. Relocating based solely on astrocartography**
Don't uproot your life solely because a planetary line runs through a city. Factor in practical realities. Visit first. Astrocartography is a tool, not a directive.

**5. Forgetting about transits**
Your astrocartography map is fixed (based on your birth chart). But transiting planets interact with your relocated chart over time. A city might feel different during different periods.

**6. Comparing charts with others**
Your ♃ Jupiter line doesn't run through the same places as someone else's. Their map is irrelevant to yours.

**7. Dismissing ♄ Saturn entirely**
♄ Saturn lines aren't punishment. They're places where you grow through effort and discipline. Some of the most meaningful life chapters happen under ♄ Saturn lines. The question isn't "is ♄ Saturn there?" but "am I ready for what ♄ Saturn asks?"

**8. Skipping the relocated chart**
The astrocartography map is the overview. The relocated chart is the detail. For serious relocation decisions, use both.

## 15. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Angle** | One of four key chart points (MC, IC, AC, DC) where planetary energy manifests in specific life areas |
| **Ascendant (AC)** | Eastern horizon point. Governs self-image, identity, first impressions. Also called the Rising Sign |
| **Astrocartography** | Branch of astrology mapping your birth chart onto the globe, showing where planetary lines fall geographically |
| **Descendant (DC)** | Western horizon point, opposite the Ascendant. Governs relationships and partnerships |
| **IC (Imum Coeli)** | Lowest chart point, opposite the Midheaven. Governs home, roots, inner life. Also called the Nadir |
| **Line crossing** | Where two planetary lines intersect, combining both energies. Also called a paran |
| **MC (Midheaven)** | Highest chart point. Governs career, public life, reputation |
| **Natal chart** | Your birth chart, calculated for the exact time and place of birth |
| **Orb** | The distance from an exact line within which influence is felt |
| **Paran** | Short for paranatellontae. A latitude line created where two planetary lines cross |
| **Planetary line** | A line on the astrocartography map showing where a planet was at a specific angle at birth |
| **Power zone** | Area around a line crossing where both planetary energies are active (~100-150 miles) |
| **Relocated chart** | Natal chart recalculated for a different location. Planets stay in same signs; houses and angles shift |
| **Transit** | Current position of a planet, which interacts with your natal and relocated chart over time |

## What to Do Next

You now have the knowledge to read your own astrocartography chart. You understand the angles, the planets, every major combination, distance rules, line crossings, relocated charts, and how to put it all together into a practical evaluation.

As a first step: pull up your chart and find the city you currently live in. Read the lines. See if it matches your experience. That tends to be the moment where this shifts from theory to something personal.

If you're seriously considering a move, work through the framework in Section 11 and use the worksheets. Take your time.

Your chart is a map. Where you go is still your choice.
